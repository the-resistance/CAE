# Abstract

The Convergent Archetype Framework (CAF) proposes a structured meta-model for aggregating independent symbolic classification systems into a unified compatibility and trait convergence engine.

Rather than relying on a single astrological or symbolic system, CAF measures cross-domain agreement and disagreement to quantify archetypal reinforcement and structural compatibility.

---

# Introduction

Symbolic systems such as Western astrology, Vedic astrology, Chinese zodiac, and numerology have historically operated independently.

CAF introduces a structural convergence methodology:

Agreement across independent systems increases signal strength.

Disagreement identifies internal tension or compatibility friction.

---

# Theoretical Foundation

CAF rests on four principles:

1. Domain Independence  
2. Weighted Aggregation  
3. Multi-System Alignment Theory (MSA)  
4. Confidence Separation  

Each domain functions as an independent classifier.

---

# Multi-System Alignment Theory (MSA)

If multiple independent systems attribute the same trait to an individual, the likelihood of reinforced archetypal expression increases.

TraitStrength = SupportingDomains / ActiveDomains

This produces measurable convergence.

# Compatibility Modeling

Compatibility is calculated via:

- Normalized weighted averaging
- Non-normalized structural summation
- Confidence calibration

This ensures reproducibility and structural transparency.
